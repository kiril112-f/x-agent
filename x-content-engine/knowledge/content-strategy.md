# Content strategy — provisional

Primary external methodology: `eps-content-playbook.md`. Use it for reader value, packaging, simplicity, experience-based substance, compression and swipe-file decisions. Verify its time-sensitive platform claims separately.

## Content pillars

Initial mix to test, not a permanent quota:

1. Product building / build in public — 30%.
   App research, decisions, MVPs, launches, metrics, failures and iteration.
2. Distribution / marketing / social systems — 25%.
   Short-form, UGC, influencer campaigns, X mechanics, funnels and channel lessons.
3. AI tools / workflows / automation — 20%.
   Skills, prompts, image/video models, coding agents and personal systems.
4. Founder cases / curated signal / relevant news — 15%.
   Other builders, Indie Hackers cases, model launches and useful market examples.
5. Personal operating system — 10%.
   Discipline, planning, sport, productivity and relevant personal achievements.

## Exclusions

- politics;
- unrelated celebrity or outrage news;
- random brainrot;
- personal disputes, school, relationships or everyday diary filler;
- topics with no link to online business, products, AI, marketing or self-development.

## Formats

Prioritize:

- original short and medium posts;
- quote-post + concrete extension or personal comment;
- Reddit-style breakdowns;
- founder/app case studies;
- low-effort document visuals and screenshots;
- long X Articles when the idea truly needs depth;
- occasional personal story with a useful lesson.

Target roughly 4–5 valuable original posts per week. Lightweight quote-posts or short reactions may appear more often when there is real signal. Do not enforce a rigid daily count.

Promotional content: normally no more than once per week and only when contextually earned.

## Creation model

Most posts begin with Kirill's draft, reference or idea. The agent may independently surface promising cases and news, but every draft goes through human review. Reference-led content must add original analysis and attribution; never swap nouns in someone else's viral post.

Before a research-led draft, build the context/resource pack from `context-storytelling.md`. Strong posts should connect the idea to real people, tools, products, cases or primary resources when those anchors improve understanding. Do not force mentions into personal posts that already have a real artifact.

Use storytelling to expose cause and effect: what someone expected, what they did, what happened, why, and what changes next. Never invent a narrative to make ordinary information feel dramatic.

Before final delivery, run the no-fluff compression contract from `eps-content-playbook.md`. Research depth must not leak into the post as unnecessary background or secondary scenarios.

## Проверка распределения в X

Перед выбором упаковки использовать `x-algorithm-playbook.md`. Уточнить конкретного читателя, payoff и причину естественного взаимодействия; сохранить исходный тезис Кирилла. Устойчивые pillars дают редакционную связность, но не являются доказанным «бонусом за нишу». Частота 4–5 постов — рабочий ориентир по ресурсам, не требование алгоритма.

Результаты проверять по `../operations/algorithm-review-loop.md`: сравнимые snapshots, нормализованные метрики, одна гипотеза на серию и реальные целевые outcomes. Article-корпус показывает примеры, но не устанавливает причинность и не обещает повторение просмотров.
