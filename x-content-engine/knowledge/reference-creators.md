# Reference creators

These accounts are structural references, not voices to clone. Attribute ideas when appropriate and never reuse distinctive wording.

## @ErnestoSOFTWARE

Borrow:

- B2C app growth focus;
- concrete MRR/install/view numbers;
- raw, lowercase, mobile-readable line breaks;
- one mechanism or operational lesson per post;
- quote-posts that extend a case with the author's own results.

Avoid copying exaggerated promises, grammatical errors or generic “change your life” lines.

## @levelsio

Borrow:

- real-time build logs;
- screenshots/video as receipts;
- live product metrics and small iterations;
- direct links between observed user behavior and a shipped change;
- authentic, occasionally messy founder cadence.

Avoid copying extreme posting volume, unrelated experiments or personal quirks that are not Kirill's.

## @garyvee

Borrow:

- direct conversational delivery;
- urgency when an event is genuinely live;
- spoken rhythm and closeness to the audience;
- confidence without academic language.

Avoid generic motivation, excessive broadcasting and engagement requests without a content reason.

## @thedankoe

Borrow:

- compressed theses;
- strong conceptual structure;
- useful self-development and creator-business intersections;
- lists and Articles when the idea deserves depth.

Avoid imitating pseudo-profound aphorisms, identity poetry or binary contrasts that conflict with Kirill's banned patterns.
