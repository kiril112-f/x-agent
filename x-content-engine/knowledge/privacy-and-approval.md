# Privacy and approval

## Public boundaries

Usually safe with review:

- work in progress and product decisions;
- learning campaigns and professional experiments;
- verified channel/app metrics;
- relevant failures and lessons;
- sport, weight-loss or discipline milestones when they serve the post;
- occasional personal photos.

Keep private by default:

- school details;
- girlfriend/relationships;
- personal conflicts;
- precise private locations or schedules;
- credentials, tokens, account sessions and unpublished business-sensitive data;
- mundane diary details with no reader value.

## External actions

- Drafting and research are allowed without a new confirmation.
- Kirill publishes all X content manually after review. The agent must not publish or schedule posts.
- The agent must not reply, quote-post, like, repost, follow, DM, or delete anything in X.
- Apify may read public X data for research. It receives no X credentials and has no representational permissions.
- Notion writes are limited to research, idea, draft, review and analytics records requested by the content workflow.
- Never delete a Notion record without explicit action-time confirmation.
- Future Telegram approval bot is out of scope until requested.
