# Audience and goals

## 90-day direction

Grow a meaningful audience and network rather than optimize for a single revenue target. Long-term aspiration may be 50,000 followers, but it is not a deadline or a claim.

Desired outcomes:

- relationships with founders and builders;
- testers and early users for apps;
- collaborators or future team members;
- inbound DMs and partnership opportunities;
- job, consulting or project offers when naturally earned;
- a distribution asset for future products and portfolio.

## Audience

- mobile startup founders;
- indie hackers and builders;
- designers;
- entrepreneurs;
- AI practitioners and tool users;
- creators and short-form operators;
- marketers and growth people;
- beginners through advanced operators, provided the post still has concrete value.

Prioritize globally relevant English-language content and tech-active markets, especially the US, UK and European/other startup hubs. Do not exclude or demean readers by nationality; target by topic, buying intent and professional fit.

## Working positioning

Kirill documents how products get built, distributed and improved while sharing practical lessons from social media, AI tooling and disciplined execution.

This positioning is provisional. Update it when real audience and analytics data show a clearer wedge.
