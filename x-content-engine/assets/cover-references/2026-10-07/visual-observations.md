# Visual observations from the downloaded originals

These are direct visual observations for design study, not claims about performance or how the source creator produced an image. Numbers on reference covers are the source authors' claims, not verified proof for Kirill's future covers. IDs below match the original user link order and `manifest.json`.

| Family | References | Visible grammar | Good learning target | Risk when adapting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monochrome narrative engraving | 03, 09, 15, 16, 22 | Dense ink-like detail, very large light/dark masses, small human scale or a single event; little/no headline | One legible metaphor even without text | Noise at thumbnail size; too many equally bright objects |
| Giant type plus physical object | 04, 06, 10, 11, 23, 31 | Huge white type; one saturated or black field; object deliberately interrupts parts of lettering | Scale contrast and clear spatial overlap | Object hides the semantic core or feels pasted on |
| Editorial currency / collage | 02, 12 | Paper edges, banknote engraving, serif type; warm or monochrome palette | Text integrated with a material surface | Fake financial proof or illegible tiny banknote text |
| Minimal symbol equation | 07, 08 | Two/three recognizable symbols with plus/equal signs; ample empty space | Immediate conceptual reduction | Generic clipart with no specific idea; incorrect logos |
| Metric / proof panel | 14, 25 | 14: dark chart with highlighted metric; 25: light neutral field, two large metrics, slim divider | Sparse aligned hierarchy and actual evidence | Invented metrics masquerading as evidence |
| Documentary screenshot | 28 | Dominant phone/screen, code background, proof shown rather than restated | Real artifact and intentional crop | Generative UI text or unreadable tiny evidence |
| Hand-drawn explainer | 26 | Whiteboard, black strokes, red emphasis, simple sequential boxes | Clear ordered mechanism | Extra arrows and decorative scribbles undermine meaning |
| Systems map | 20, 27 | Thin connectors, few repeated nodes, exact labels; dark technical or white academic surface | Real structure with consistent labels | Diagram too dense to survive feed-scale rendering |
| Cinematic / character subject | 01, 05, 19 | One dominant subject in a coherent world; 01 is an orange illustrated character scene; 05/19 use directional light and physical product/metaphor | Specific subject explains the topic | Arbitrary props, irrelevant characters or excessive lighting |
| Warm editorial illustration | 17 | Cream paper, orange linework, bold condensed serif heading, meaningful exaggerated scale | Distinct print-like vocabulary | Faux vintage decoration without narrative relevance |
| Evidence plus cards | 18 | Left headline, right fan of three phone frames, orange accent, subdued technical field | Short title + visible example set | Tiny proof badges and duplicated ornamental panels |
| Halftone energy / platform convergence | 13, 21 | Strong central vanishing point or gesture, colored beams/halftone, platform symbols | A clear direction of attention | Excessive glow and high-frequency texture become generic AI imagery |

All 28 downloaded covers have an approximately 2.5:1 aspect ratio. This is an observation of this corpus; not an independently checked current upload rule.

Reference 05 title says 20m+ while cover says 23M; preserve the original source artifact, but do not reconcile or reuse either number as a verified fact. Reference 15 and 09 use a similar falling-into-vortex composition, demonstrating a shared visual grammar; it does not prove the same creator/tool or license.

Potential practice brief: one consistent hypothetical topic, no invented metrics, with three distinct routes — monochrome metaphor; giant type with one object; editorial diagram. Inspect the object, thumbnail and exact typography separately. Use independently typeset lettering for new covers, retaining image generation for image components only when it improves the result.
