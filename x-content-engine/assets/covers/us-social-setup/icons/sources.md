# Logo sources

The three icon files are original app artwork retrieved through Apple's official iTunes Lookup service on 2026-10-05. Complete returned public metadata, including artwork URLs, is in `itunes-lookup.json`.

- TikTok: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id835599320
- Instagram: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id389801252
- YouTube: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id544007664
- Lookup: https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=835599320,389801252,544007664&country=us

The USWDS flag asset is unused in the final cover. The final background comes from the phone photo supplied in the user's Notion draft. The original photograph is preserved; only the separate cover derivative uses blur and dimming.
