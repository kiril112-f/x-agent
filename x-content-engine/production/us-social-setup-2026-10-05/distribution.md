## Distribution checks

- Share the guide with an existing relevant community only where it is welcome; no unsolicited mass DMs or scripted engagement.
- Capture comparable snapshots around 24 hours and seven days after publication if useful. Record impressions, substantive replies, profile visits/follows and saves only when the data is available. Do not schedule automated collection as part of this task.
