# Draft-to-post evaluation rubric

Score 0–2:

1. Thesis fidelity.
2. Factual support and uncertainty handling.
3. Brand voice match.
4. Specificity and concrete detail.
5. Hook/payoff honesty.
6. X-native mobile readability.
7. Paragraph rhythm and whitespace preservation.
8. Information density and absence of redundancy.
9. Human cadence and no generic AI prose.
10. Context richness and responsible attribution.
11. Proportional edit that still feels authored by Kirill.

Pass: at least 19/22 and no hard failure from the skill quality gate.

Mechanical lint supplements this rubric; it cannot judge writing quality or authorship.
