# Case 01 — blocked unsupported claim

## Input

> This guy built a $2B company with 2 people. And the craziest part? He only used AI. So what's your next excuse bro 😂

## Result

`BLOCKED — needs source/confirmation`

The company is not named, the valuation date is missing, and “only used AI” may describe a build method rather than literally every company function. A polished version would turn three uncertain claims into confident misinformation.

Research needed:

1. Company and founders.
2. Primary source for the $2B valuation.
3. Actual team size at that date.
4. What work AI performed.

No publishable rewrite produced.
