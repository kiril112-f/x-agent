# Art direction for article covers

Read `x-content-engine/knowledge/article-cover-styles.md` from project root. Resolve exact images through the manifest and open them. A title or palette does not describe the visual grammar.

## Choose from the article's mechanism

- Practical procedure: a clear product or type/object composition, simple system or whiteboard.
- Verified outcome: documentary proof or sparse metric panel, with real evidence only.
- Attention, discipline, choice, uncertainty: monochrome narrative engraving with one tension.
- Production/economic mechanism: warm editorial engraving or controlled material collage.
- Entertainment/product content: cinematic object, purposeful character scene or real evidence cards.

These are candidates, not mandatory mappings. A monochrome scene can explain a concrete workflow. Show 2–3 relevant images and ask which style the user wants before a new Article, unless already chosen or exploration explicitly delegated.

## Translate the reference

Record subject, type/object scale, anchor positions, light/dark masses, text role, font category, material, palette, depth and crop. Carry over grammar, not another author's artwork, claims or partnership cues.

Use one visual sentence: «A person crosses a narrow bridge toward one opening»; not «AI, growth, productivity and success». Derive the metaphor from the article. Do not repeat vortices for every abstract subject.

## Kirill's type-led preference

Giant lettering and foreground objects should form one composition. Match the reference's relative scale and hierarchy before adjusting color. Design the depth in advance: background → typography → a meaningful foreground object → optional restrained highlights/shadow. Objects may sit behind one part of a lockup and in front of another when the compositor supports it.

Avoid a small generic icon beside two equal-sized text lines. A play triangle is not a substitute for TikTok when the topic is TikTok. Use real brand assets for real entities; create an original setting, object treatment or metaphor around them. Save the source. If a generated casing is useful, composite the genuine mark onto its face separately.

Occlusion must look intentional and keep the word instantly readable. Start with the bottom of a few central letters, not the opening letter/counter of the key word. The lab's v2 covered the `p` in playbook and was revised. Exact overlap percentages are useful only for the chosen font/object geometry, not universal quality scores.

## Composition and fonts

- Art-led panorama: figure in one third, destination/opposing mass in the other. No headline required if the article title supplies the subject.
- Type-led: deliberately unequal scales, short dominant word, controlled overlap. Heavy grotesk; no forced extrusion on every letter. Depth can come from occlusion and physical objects.
- Editorial split: serif/italic over quiet paper, illustration right/bottom. Integrate the art with the text edge or foreground where it helps the selected direction.
- System/proof: exact readable labels or real evidence. A dense map belongs inside the Article. Offer this restrained route explicitly; do not substitute it silently for a requested dramatic cover.

Use actual installed/licensed fonts and save family/file/weight. No horizontal stretching. Choose line breaks semantically, then adjust size/tracking using visible glyph bounds. Serif is not automatically more premium than sans; choose from material and tone.

## Image brief

Specify subject, medium, framing, palette, reserved copy zone, crop-sensitive edges and exclusions. Generate no exact text/numbers/UI. Request real alpha for cutouts. Do not ask a model to invent logos, metrics or proof.

The lab tests a rust copperplate press with a quiet left copy zone, a monochrome bridge metaphor and a blank glass casing with separately inserted genuine app artwork. Prompts and original assets live in `cover-lab-2026-10-07/assets/`.

Use Pinterest/web only when the local library lacks a needed direction or discovery is requested. Trace original creator/source where possible; Pinterest is not automatically the creator or license. Do not add visual clutter merely because a reference looked impressive.
