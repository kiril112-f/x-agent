# Source policy

Prefer, in order:

1. Official documentation, source code, company/product announcement, filings or first-party data.
2. Original interview, talk, dataset, paper or directly attributable creator post.
3. Reputable reporting that links to its evidence.
4. Practitioner commentary as context, clearly labelled as opinion or heuristic.

Rules:

- Fresh or unstable claims require a current lookup.
- Technical claims use primary sources.
- Use exact dates for news and distinguish publication date from event date.
- Do not quote more than needed; paraphrase accurately and link the source.
- If a source cannot establish the claim, narrow or remove the claim.
- Never convert an estimate into a fact.
- Algorithm evidence must identify repository, commit SHA, code path, formula/guards, and observation date. Published defaults are not proof of every live configuration. Old Twitter weights and mixed-vintage growth guides are historical references, not current instructions; use `x-content-engine/knowledge/x-algorithm-playbook.md` and its audit.
