# Evidence ledger

For each material external claim record:

```text
Claim:
Source URL:
Source owner/type:
Published/event date:
Confidence: high | medium | low
Allowed wording:
Caveat:
```

Research brief format:

1. What happened — 2–5 bullets.
2. Why it matters for Kirill's audience — mechanism, not hype.
3. Best content angle — one recommendation.
4. Evidence ledger.
5. People/entities and resource map (`name`, `URL`, `why useful`).
6. Counterpoint or exception.
7. Kirill's original contribution and attribution plan.
8. Open questions or blocked claims.
